Scope: the qcore-solutions.com landing page, new-world variant B, built beside variant A (same look as the current site) so the user can pick one. Mode: Persuade.

Audience and action: SME owners, finance and ops leads (Egypt first, GCC too) deciding whether to talk to an ERPNext partner. Primary action: WhatsApp (wa.me/201505716467) or email (contact@qcore-solutions.com). ## Direction contract

THESIS: The rollout is a metro line you can ride: delivery phases are stations, services are lines, integrations are interchanges. Refuses the split hero with a dashboard screenshot and a row of service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cool map-white ground, Qcore navy ink (#0e1622) and Qcore blue (#087afe) as the trunk line; three more line inks (red, amber, green), each owned by one service. Thick strokes turning only at 45°, white station roundels with navy rings, enamel-sign panels (navy, rounded, white type) for headlines. Overpass (signage) for Latin, Alexandria for Arabic. Signs follow the EN/AR toggle: one language at a time (user decision, 2026-10-21: "the english version has arabic text as well").

STORY: The visitor sees the whole delivery route at once, learns each station is run by a named consultant, sees where those consultants have delivered before, and boards via WhatsApp or email.

The destination-board panel sits top-left (about 45% wide) with an Arabic line, the H1, one sentence, and WhatsApp + email buttons.
