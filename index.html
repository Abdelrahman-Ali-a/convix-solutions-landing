How a rollout actually runs

TYPICAL 10–14 WEEKS
01 · WK 1–2

Process mapping

We sit with your team, document how work moves today, and write down every gap before touching a setting.

02 · WK 3–6

Configure & migrate

Masters, workflows and opening balances loaded. Standard first — customisation only where it earns its cost.

03 · WK 7–9

UAT & training

Your people run their own real transactions on a test copy, with role-based training and written SOPs.

04 · WK 10+

Go-live & two closes

We stay through two month-end closes, then hand over to support with everything documented.

Straight answers, before the call

Do you host it, or do we?

Either. Most clients run on their own cloud account so the data and the backups stay theirs; we can manage it or hand you the keys.

What if ERPNext doesn’t cover something?

We build it as a proper app on top rather than hacking core, so your upgrades keep working. That gap list is written before kickoff, not discovered in month four.

How much of our time will this take?

Expect one process owner per area for a few hours a week, and a heavier fortnight during UAT. We do the configuration and the data work.

Can you take over a stalled implementation?

Yes — we start with an audit of what’s configured and what’s broken, then give you a written plan to finish or to reset.

Tell us what’s slowing you down

A 30-minute call, no deck. We’ll tell you whether ERPNext is the right answer — including when it isn’t.
