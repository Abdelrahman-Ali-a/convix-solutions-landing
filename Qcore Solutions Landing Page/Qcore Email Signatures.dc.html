Before rolling out

  • The logo must live at a public HTTPS URL (e.g. corvixsolutions.com/sig/logo.png) — mail clients won't load a local file. Upload it at 2× the display width for retina.
  • No web fonts: Gmail and Outlook strip them, so everything is Arial/Helvetica by design.
  • Keep the signature under ~650px wide so it never forces horizontal scroll on mobile.
  • Skip social icons unless the accounts are active — dead links cost more trust than they earn.
