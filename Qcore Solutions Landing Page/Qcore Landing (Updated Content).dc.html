Questions we get every week

Straight answers, before the call.

Is ERPNext really free?

The software is open source, so there are no per-user licence fees. You pay for hosting and for the work — implementation, customisation, support. That is usually where the savings against a licensed ERP show up.

Can it handle our manufacturing?

Multi-level BOMs, subcontracting, job cards and capacity planning are standard. Where a process is genuinely unusual, we build it as a Frappe app rather than bending the core.

What happens to our old data?

Ledgers, stock balances and open documents are migrated and reconciled against your existing books before go-live. Your auditor gets a report, not a promise.

Who owns the customisations?

You do. Code sits in your repository, the system runs on your server or cloud account, and nothing we write is locked to us.

Do you support us after go-live?

Yes — an SLA-backed desk, version upgrades and an agreed pool of change requests each month. Most clients move onto that the week hypercare ends.

We need a mobile app too. One vendor or two?

One. The same team that configures your ERP builds the field, warehouse and approval apps that read from it, so nothing gets lost between vendors.

Thirty minutes, no slide deck. We'll tell you honestly what to configure, what to build, and what to leave alone.
